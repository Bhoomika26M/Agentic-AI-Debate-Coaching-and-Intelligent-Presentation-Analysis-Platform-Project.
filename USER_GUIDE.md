## 2. Learner Workflow

### Starting an AI Debate
1. Navigate to **Create Debate** in the sidebar.
2. Enter a motion (e.g. *"Should artificial intelligence replace traditional education?"*) or click any recommended resolution prompt.
3. Choose your Stance (*For, Against, Neutral*), Format (*Oxford, Parliamentary, Policy*), and AI Opponent Personality (*Analytical, Aggressive, Socratic, etc.*).
4. Click **Initialize Debate Room**.

### In the Live Debate Room
1. Read the AI opponent's opening thesis.
2. Deliver your argument by typing into the bottom input or clicking the **Microphone** icon to dictate via voice.
3. Click **Send** (or hit Enter).
4. Watch the right-hand **AI Coaching Panel**:
   * Inspect detected **Logical Fallacies** (expand the badge to see why it's problematic and view an improved version).
   * Review real-time criteria scores.
   * Read the AI opponent's dynamic counter-speech.
5. Conclude the debate at any time by clicking **End Debate & View Scorecard**.

### Reviewing Scorecards & Exports
1. View the large **Overall Score** with the exact 30/20/20/15/15 weighted breakdown:
   * Argument Quality (30%)
   * Evidence Usage (20%)
   * Logical Consistency (20%)
   * Rebuttal Precision (15%)
   * Communication (15%)
2. Click **Download PDF Report** or **Export Excel (.xlsx)** to download your report file.

### Speech & Presentation Lab
1. Go to **Presentation Lab** in the sidebar.
2. Record live speech via your microphone or upload an audio file.
3. View speaking tempo in **Words Per Minute (WPM)**, **Filler Words Breakdown** (`um`, `like`, `you know`), and **Vocal Confidence Score** (0–100).

### Practice Drills & Exercises
1. Navigate to **Practice Drills**.
2. Filter by category: *Fallacy, Argument, Evidence, Rebuttal, Presentation*.
3. Submit your response to receive immediate AI evaluation, numerical score, and an exemplary benchmark answer.

## 3. Debate Coach Portal
1. View cohort statistics, active student rosters, and cohort skill gaps.
2. Review student debate sessions in **Debate Evaluations**.
3. Add qualitative coaching notes and assign remedial drills.

---

## 4. Educator Portal
1. Inspect class mean scores, rank leaders, and grade distributions.
2. Identify systemic curriculum weaknesses (e.g. empirical citations).
3. Export class-wide cohort summary reports for faculty review.

---

## 5. Administrator Portal
1. Search and filter all registered users in **User Management**.
2. Change user roles or activate/deactivate accounts.
3. Monitor inference request volume, latency timelines (ms), and server status in **AI Telemetry**.
